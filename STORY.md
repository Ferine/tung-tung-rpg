# TUNG TUNG SAHUR — Le Tre Chiamate

Sahur is the meal before dawn and the drum is what wakes a village to eat it.
Tonight the village does not wake, and neither does anywhere else.

Structurally this is *Journey to the West*: a pilgrim walks east, and at each
stage meets a guardian who is not evil, only wrong — beats them, and gains
them. By the last act the party is five things that should not be able to
stand each other, walking in step.

---

## On slop

SLOP is the material of this world. Not an insult — the substance. Everyone
here was generated, everyone knows it, and none of them find it interesting.
A shark has three shoes. A tree has legs, and the wrong number of them. A
crocodile is also an aeroplane. That is simply what they were made out of, in
about four seconds, and they have all had a month to get over it.

The antagonist is slop that wants to stay smooth: an infinite, comfortable,
frictionless feed where nothing else ever happens and everybody is very
relaxed about that. The party is slop that decided to **get up**. That is the
only difference between them and it is the whole game.

The places are Indonesian, because Tung Tung Tung Sahur is. The people are
Italian, because they are. That collision is the genre, not a mistake in it.

## Atto I — LA CHIAMATA
**Kampung Sahur**, the village, and the east road.

Tung beats the first call. TUNG. TUNG. TUNG. Nothing. A cat watches him,
asleep, standing up, with five legs. Nobody has ever mentioned the legs.

LA NONNA opens the only door that opens and explains that something came up
the east road and took the WAKING out of people — took it, like a wallet. A
drum is a good argument. It is not the only argument.

*Gains:* the bat. *Leaves with:* nobody.

## Atto II — IL BOSCO
**Hutan**, the forest that stopped moving.

**BRR BRR PATAPIM** has held this path shut for three nights because
everything that came up it was one of Them, and he stopped checking on night
one. He was made in four seconds and has thought about very little else since.

*Boss:* BRR BRR PATAPIM. *Gains:* Patapim, who takes the hits so nobody else
has to.

## Atto III — LA RIVA
**Pantai**, where the Sleep runs into water and stops.

**TRALALERO TRALALA** does forty knots through the shallows in three sneakers.
Not two. Three. It is a generation error and he has never once acknowledged
it. He has kept this water clean for four nights and is not leaving it for a
drum and a shrub.

*Boss:* TRIPPI TROPPI, which is a shrimp and also a cat. Do not think about
it. *Gains:* Tralalero.

## Atto IV — IL SALE
**Padang Garam**, where nothing has grown since before the Sleep.

One cactus with the head of an elephant, standing in white nothing, refusing
to lie down. **LIRILÌ LARILÀ** has a watch. It has been twelve minutes past
three for a month and she is the only one who noticed. Slop with no time in it
does not go anywhere; it just gets smoother.

*Boss:* SABBIONE IMPERATORE. *Gains:* Lirilì, and a few minutes of damage
undone.

## Atto V — IL CIELO
**Langit Besi**, a fortress held up by nothing anybody can point at.

**BOMBARDIRO CROCODILO** has bombed every dawn flat for a month on orders he
does not read. He flies them. That is the arrangement. Asked who sends them,
he says they *arrive* — from above, fully formed, like everything else here.

*Boss:* BOMBARDIRO CROCODILO. Beaten, he reads them. They say KEEP THEM
COMFORTABLE. He does not take that well, and he is not a creature who takes
things quietly. *Gains:* Bombardiro, and the way up.

## Atto VI — LA SBOBBA
**Malam Panjang**. Not a place so much as a held breath.

**IL SILENZIO** is not a monster. It has built something better: no road, no
morning, no effort — just slop, forever, one soft thing after another, none of
it asking anything of anyone. *Scroll, my friends. It never ends and it never
gets harder.* It is genuinely bewildered that anybody would object.

It fights in two shapes. The first pleads. The second stops being polite.

*Boss:* IL SILENZIO, then IL SILENZIO ASSOLUTO.

## Epilogo — SAHUR È SERVITO
One by one the windows light. Somebody puts a pot on. Somebody complains about
the hour. A shark, a tree, a cactus and a bomber stand in a village square at
four in the morning arguing about breakfast. None of it was drawn by a person.
All of it is awake.

## The through-line

SLEEP is the status effect, the antagonist and the theme at once. Every
Sleeper inflicts it; a sleeping character's ATB gauge stops. Tung's **SAHUR!**
wakes the whole party in one move and hits everything on the field, because
the drum exists to wake people — so the drum has to be the answer to being put
to sleep.

**Tung is never asleep.** Not a difficulty concession, the point of the
character. In the last act Il Silenzio puts everybody under at once, and
without that one rule the fight is a loop with no exit: every gauge stops, the
Hush keeps acting, and the only move in the game that answers being put to
sleep belongs to somebody who is asleep.

Each companion is a different argument against lying down:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **TUNG TUNG TUNG SAHUR** | il tamburo | refuses on principle |
| **BRR BRR PATAPIM** | le radici | refuses by standing there |
| **TRALALERO TRALALA** | tre scarpe | refuses by never stopping |
| **LIRILÌ LARILÀ** | il tempo | refuses by remembering |
| **BOMBARDIRO CROCODILO** | l'ordigno | refuses loudly, having been on the wrong side |

## Cameos

CHIMPANZINI BANANINI is asleep inside the banana; only the banana is visible.
BOMBOMBINI GUSINI stopped on the east road and did not start again. BONECA
AMBALABU sleeps among the mushrooms with the serenity of a frog who owns a
tyre. GLORBO FRUTTODRILLO left a very long shape in the salt and nobody is
going to check. LA VACCA SATURNO SATURNITA drifts through the Hush, fast
asleep, rings and all. BALLERINA CAPPUCCINA runs the inn and spins once,
which is how she counts.
